## Suggested order

1. **#1, #2, #3 together: the first upload and the identity join.** They are one code path in `src/sync.ts` (`push` and `restart`). Today a device with more than 38 rows, or whose first sync failed, silently never reaches the server, and a passkey sign-in on a new browser wipes the journal key and every past line with it. The device check in `Verify_List.md` (slice 14) will hit all three, and #1 already has a failing test. Fix before anyone signs in on a second device.
2. **#13: title cap and the 400 path.** A single long paste throws away up to 38 valid edits. A 500-character clamp in core plus "don't discard the batch" is small.
3. **#31 and #32: the scheduler's CPU and the `sends` table.** #31 is the only finding that stops every nudge for every user at once; caching the formatter and sending before planning is a few lines. #32 grows quietly until D1's daily read cap takes sync down with it. Fix both before the bot goes live.
4. **#4 and #5: time zone and Focus `done_at`.** Small fixes, both wrong in daily use: nudges at the wrong hour away from UTC+8, and finished steps reappearing in Today.
5. **#22 and #7: focus after a control disappears, and the stale next-action dialog.** The highest-traffic accessibility gap and a one-line duplicate-step bug, before the keyboard and screen-reader pass in Verify.
